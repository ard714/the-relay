How it works


Apply with the form. Use an email address you check often.

You get the assignment by email within a few minutes. It comes from "Hiring Team" with a PDF attached. Check your spam folder if it isn't in your inbox. The deadline is in the email: 4 days from the moment it is sent, so apply when you can set aside the time.

Work alone. You can use any AI tool. We want to see how you direct it and what in the submission is yours.

Submit with the submission form linked in that email.

Share one Drive folder containing your write-up, your code and results.csv.

Share the folder so anyone with the link can view it.

Use the same email address you applied with. We match your submission to your application by email.



You get a confirmation email when your submission is received. Submissions after the deadline are marked late.

Next steps. Shortlisted candidates get a live conversation where you explain and change your system. After that comes a team chat.

Three notes on the automation:


Folder sharing. The script can only read a folder that is shared. "Anyone with the link" is the safest setting. Without it, the submission fails with a "No Drive link found" or unreadable-file message.

Matching by email. The email on the submission form must match the application form. A typo gives "No applicant with this email" on the sheet.

File name. The file must be named exactly results.csv. The assignment email now highlights that name too.
